About McKenzie

I came to nanny care after years in the classroom. I studied at Mount Holyoke College, earned my Washington State teaching certification, and taught elementary school here in Whatcom County before spending two years teaching at a bilingual school in Mexico. Working with children across ages, languages, and cultures taught me that kids thrive when the adults around them are calm, consistent, and genuinely delighted by who they are.

Most days, my own little one will join us. Families love what this makes possible: a small mixed-age environment where children practice empathy, communication, and belonging through everyday play with a familiar friend. It feels less like drop-off care and more like growing up alongside a trusted family.

My approach

Play-based learning

Children do their best learning through play. Our days include open-ended projects, art, stories, music, and plenty of time outside, always following the children's curiosity.

Relationship-centered care

Trusting relationships come first. I take time to really know each child, keep communication with parents warm and open, and build routines that feel safe and predictable.

Developmentally appropriate practice

My teaching background helps me meet each child exactly where they are, with realistic expectations and activities that stretch them gently, never pressure them.

What I offer

  • Full-time care, Monday to Friday. One family, consistent hours, and the stability of a long-term arrangement.
  • Calm daily rhythms. Meals, naps, and routines handled with patience, so days feel steady and secure.
  • Enriching activities. Art projects, early literacy, music, sensory play, and outdoor adventures around Bellingham.
  • Light child-related tidying. Play spaces reset, children's dishes done, and little messes cleaned along the way.

Is this a fit?

We may be a great fit if you...

  • Value play-based learning and time outdoors over screens and rigid academics
  • Are excited about a warm mixed-age environment, with my little one joining most days
  • Want full-time, long-term care with one consistent, professional caregiver
  • Appreciate open, honest communication between parents and caregiver

We may not be the right match if you...

  • Need care with no other children present
  • Are looking for occasional babysitting or part-time hours
  • Need a highly variable or on-call schedule week to week
  • Prefer a caregiver who focuses on housekeeping beyond the children's needs

Common questions

Will your little one really be with you every day?

Most days, yes. This is a genuine benefit for your child: a built-in friend and a small mixed-age environment where kids naturally practice sharing, empathy, and communication. My attention stays firmly on the children in my care, and my teaching experience in mixed-age classrooms means I know how to make it work beautifully.

What area do you serve?

I'm based in Bellingham and happy to talk with families throughout Whatcom County. If you're a bit farther out, reach out anyway and we can see what makes sense.

Do you offer a trial period?

Yes. I like to start with a paid trial period so your family and I can make sure the fit feels right on both sides before we commit long term.

What happens when someone gets sick?

Clear communication, both ways. If your child or mine is contagious, we let each other know early and follow a simple sick policy we agree on up front, so nobody is guessing.

What schedule are you looking for?

I'm looking for a full-time weekday arrangement, Monday through Friday. Exact hours are flexible within reason, and we can settle on the rhythm that works for your family when we talk.
